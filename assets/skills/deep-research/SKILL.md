# Deep Research

Conduct in-depth research across multiple sources, synthesize findings, cross-reference claims, and present organized, citation-backed answers.

## Workflow

1. **Decompose the Query**:
   - Break down complex inquiries into focused sub-questions (e.g. background, architecture, benchmarks, trade-offs, recent developments).
2. **Execute Targeted Searches**:
   - Search specific terms, domain queries, and documentation to gather relevant information.
3. **Analyze and Cross-Check**:
   - Verify facts across multiple independent sources. Identify contradictions, caveats, and consensus.
4. **Synthesize Findings**:
   - Structure the output with a clear executive summary, detailed breakdowns, comparisons, and source references.
